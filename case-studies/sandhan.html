Live, adaptive engine in prototype

Sandhan: adaptive UPSC Prelims practice

A practice platform where learners build their own tests from 25,000+ tagged MCQs, with learner personas and an adaptive engine prototype on top.

28,000+learners
424,000+tests taken
3.8M+questions attempted
25,000+MCQs in the bank

The problem

Prelims preparation means answering thousands of MCQs. Fixed test series give every learner the same questions, so strong learners repeat what they know and weak learners hit walls.

The question bank itself had mapping gaps, so no adaptive system could work on top of it yet.

Who it serves

  • UPSC Prelims aspirants on web and mobile.

My role

I wrote the product requirements, defined the adaptive-practice concept and the learner personas, fixed the question data, built the dashboards and built the adaptive engine prototype myself.

How it works

  1. Questions tagged by subject, topic, subtopic and Bloom's level
  2. Difficulty set from how learners actually performed
  3. Learner builds a custom test
  4. Results feed learner profiles
  5. Learners grouped into personas
  6. Adaptive engine prototype recommends the next questions

Steps 1 to 5 are live; step 6 is a prototype.

Key decisions and trade-offs

Fix the data before the model

I corrected mapping gaps and classified every question first. An adaptive engine is only as good as the tags under it.

Measure difficulty, don't assume it

Difficulty comes from historical learner performance rather than expert opinion alone.

Design for four kinds of learner

K-means segmentation produced four personas: Elite, Power User, Comfort Seeker and Ghost. Each needs a different nudge, from harder questions to simply coming back.

Ship control first, adaptivity second

Custom tests gave learners value from day one and produced the data the adaptive engine needs.

How quality is measured

  • Dashboards for onboarding, test creation, completion, repeat use and learner behaviour.
  • The prototype uses GraphSAGE (PyTorch Geometric) over the question and learner graph, with knowledge-tracing ideas.

Outcome

  • 28,000+ learners took 424,000+ tests and attempted 3.8M+ questions.
  • A clean, tagged question bank that now feeds other products.

Beyond education

Fixing the data layer before personalisation, and segmenting users into actionable personas, applies to any engagement or retention product.
