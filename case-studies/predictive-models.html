Prediction for planning: Prelims cutoff, mentoring demand and qualification likelihood

Three statistical models used to plan resources and target help: a yearly Prelims cutoff forecast, a daily mentoring demand forecast and a likelihood score for clearing Prelims.

Role: Owner and modellerPeriod: 2019 to 2026Areas: Regression, Forecasting, Clustering, Survey design
3models in regular use
10,000+students in mentoring programmes
50+mentors allocated
YearlyPrelims cutoff forecast

The problem

Students want to know where they stand after the Prelims exam, long before official results.

Mentoring requests arrived through forms, email and calls with no forecast, so mentors were either overloaded or idle, and help did not always reach the students who could benefit most.

Who it serves

  • Aspirants waiting for Prelims results.
  • Mentoring and academic operations teams.

My role

I designed and built all three models, including the survey behind the cutoff forecast and the data workflows for mentoring requests, allocation, completion and feedback.

How it works

  1. Survey responses and historical exam data
  2. Cutoff estimated with regression, distribution fitting and clustering
  3. Mentoring requests tracked from request to session held
  4. Daily demand forecast and mentor allocation
  5. Prelims and test-series performance combined into a likelihood score
  6. Shortlisted students matched with mentors

Key decisions and trade-offs

Track demand to the session actually held

Requests were followed through confirmation to completed sessions, so the forecast predicts real workload, not raw interest.

Combine several performance signals

The likelihood score uses actual Prelims performance with past Prelims and Mains test-series results, so one bad test does not decide a student's support.

Use mentors where they change outcomes

Shortlisting students with a realistic chance of clearing put scarce mentor time where it mattered most.

How quality is measured

  • Each cutoff forecast can be checked against the official cutoff once results are out.
  • Demand forecasts are built on sessions actually held, so they can be checked against real mentor workload.

Outcome

  • An annual Prelims cutoff forecast, released after each exam.
  • Mentor allocation planned from forecasts across three mentoring programmes, with 50+ mentors supporting 10,000+ students.

What I would do differently

  • Publish the forecast range and its past accuracy, not a single number.
  • Add the forecasts to a live dashboard for the operations team instead of periodic reports.
