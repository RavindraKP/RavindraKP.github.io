Vision Intelligence and the AI Knowledge Repository

A chat assistant students can open on any page, answering from a repository of 1 lakh+ documents that also feeds question generation and answer evaluation.

1 lakh+documents in the knowledge repository
2,278+conversations in 30 days
9,244+messages in the same period
3 / 25free and paid questions a day

The problem

Students have doubts at all hours, and mentors cannot answer every one. The answers already existed across magazines, past papers, textbooks and lecture transcripts, but were hard to find.

The company also needed one reliable source of knowledge that several AI products could share.

Who it serves

  • Students on the current-affairs platform and other VisionIAS products.
  • Content, classroom and mentoring teams, who use what students ask to find gaps.

My role

I led both the repository and the assistant: what goes into the repository, how it is kept current, how the assistant answers and escalates, and the usage model that links it to paid plans.

How it works

  1. Current affairs, past papers, magazines, textbooks and transcripts collected
  2. Each document embedded and indexed
  3. Daily pipelines add new content
  4. Student asks a question, optionally from an article page
  5. Relevant passages retrieved and an answer written with links
  6. Unresolved questions go to a human expertHuman review

Step 6 is the human escalation point.

Key decisions and trade-offs

Build the repository as a shared asset

The same repository feeds the assistant, MCQ generation and Mains answer evaluation, so improving it improves every product.

Index on publish

Every new article or PDF published on the current-affairs platform is sent for indexing automatically, so answers stay current with no manual step.

Use the daily limit as a pricing lever

Free users get 3 questions a day and subscribers 25. The limits are settings, not code, so they can be tuned against conversion.

Answer in context

The assistant can be tied to the article a student is reading, so short questions get answers about that article.

How quality is measured

  • Answers link back to the source material, so students can check them.
  • Student questions are analysed for concept gaps and shared with content, classroom and mentoring teams.

Outcome

  • 2,278+ conversations and 9,244+ messages in a 30-day period on the current-affairs deployment alone.
  • The repository of 1 lakh+ documents in mixed formats became the foundation for three AI products.

Beyond education

A shared, always-current knowledge base behind a chat assistant is the core of most enterprise support and internal-knowledge products.
