Pilot

EvalFlow: AI assessment for K-12 schools

A dual-engine assessment system that grades handwritten school tests in humanities and STEM, and sends uncertain cases to teachers.

~90%of grading automated
~100xlower grading cost
98%Devanagari handwriting accuracy
45 → 7 daysfeedback turnaround

The problem

Teachers spend many hours grading handwritten tests, and students often get feedback weeks after the test, when it no longer helps.

Many teachers in the target schools have limited comfort with digital tools, so a dashboard-only product would not be used.

Who it serves

  • Teachers and school administrators.
  • Students in K-12 classrooms, including Hindi-medium students.

My role

I contributed to the product as part of the VisionIAS AI team, drawing on the answer-evaluation work: evaluation design, quality measurement and the human review flow.

How it works

  1. Answer sheets scanned
  2. Handwriting read, including Devanagari
  3. Sent to the humanities or STEM engine
  4. Graded against the rubric
  5. Confidence checked
  6. Low-confidence answers go to a teacherHuman review
  7. Printed feedback returned to the class

Step 6 is the human review point.

Key decisions and trade-offs

Two engines, not one

Humanities answers are judged on content and structure; STEM answers on steps and working. Separate engines let each be tuned and measured on its own terms.

Let the model say it is unsure

Answers below a confidence threshold go to a teacher. This kept automation high without asking teachers to trust every grade.

Print the feedback

Paper feedback fits how these classrooms already work, and removes the need for every teacher to learn a new tool.

Protect students by design

Student details are masked before AI processing, and bias guardrails are applied to the grading prompts.

How quality is measured

  • QWK of 0.77 to 0.81 against expert evaluators, measured the same way as the UPSC answer evaluation engine.
  • 98% handwriting accuracy on Devanagari, trained on 100,000+ scripts.
  • Share of answers graded without human review: about 90%.

Outcome

  • Grading cost reduced about 100 times; turnaround cut from 45 days to 7.
  • Piloted at InfinityVision Coaching and RDGV School.
  • Shortlisted in the NITI Aayog AI by HER Challenge (Education) and recognised at the AI Impact India Summit.

Beyond education

Confidence-based routing to humans is the standard pattern for any AI that makes decisions people must trust, from loan reviews to medical triage.
