Approach

How I work

Prototype before handoff

I build working prototypes myself, so engineering starts from something tested, not a document alone.

Built the Sandhan adaptive engine prototype before engineering work began.

Measure AI against people

AI output is scored against expert humans before release, with versioned prompts and blinded model comparisons.

36 prompt versions scored against human evaluators in CopyEval.

Keep people where stakes are high

AI drafts and people approve. Uncertain cases go to a human.

AI in Classroom went from about 60 writers to 5–6 reviewers, with every output still approved.

Tie AI to the business

Trials, usage limits and pricing are designed in, so AI features pay for themselves.

Premium current affairs: about ₹1 crore a year at 4% conversion.
