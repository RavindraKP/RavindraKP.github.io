How I work

Prototype before handoff

I build working prototypes myself, so engineering starts from something tested with users rather than a document alone.

Measure AI against people

Every AI output is scored against human experts before release, with versioned prompts and blinded model comparisons.

Keep people where stakes are high

AI drafts and people approve. Low-confidence cases go to a human, and feedback from reviewers improves the system.

Tie AI to the business

Usage limits, free trials and pricing are designed in, so an AI feature earns its running cost.
